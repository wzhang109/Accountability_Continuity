# Organizing work with AI: a source-based starting point

**Working branch, October 21, 2026. AI-assisted preparation; human review pending.**

How is work divided, connected and brought to a decision when AI changes what
one person can produce? The object is an actual work process: who needs whose
input, who integrates it, and who can resolve a tradeoff. The current object is
organizational coordination itself; individual growth is not a required focus
or outcome of this branch.

The first unit is a [bounded reading of a P&G product-development experiment](cases/pg-product-workshop-2026-10-21.md),
with [source-linked records](evidence.json) and a [version/access record](sources.json).
It is a reading exercise using another study's published account, not our own
field observation, reanalysis, replication or systematic literature review.
The [observation template](OBSERVATION_TEMPLATE.md) proposes what additional
process evidence would be needed. It contains no collected events or participants.

## The distinction to investigate

Access to another specialty's perspective, integration of contributions, and
authority to commit resources are different things. A stronger deliverable alone
does not locate a bottleneck or show whose coordination work changed. Nor does
the person pressing Submit necessarily have final authority over the project.

The case records a small experimental workflow and leaves unestablished links
explicitly unknown. A claim based on the technical/commercial orientation of an
output is distinguished from direct observation of who spoke, checked, waited
or decided. Company leaders' accounts of friction, experimental assignments and
the authors' outcome estimates also have different evidential roles.

## A bounded next question

When generating candidate work becomes easier, what happens to the work of
integrating it and deciding what can proceed? Three competing possibilities
remain open: the burden decreases, moves to another role, or grows because
more outputs need checking. These are candidate explanations for later
investigation, not findings of this project or an asserted universal pattern.

For a real process, the next useful record would link an input, a responsible
role, a handoff requirement, any observed wait or rework, and the authority for
the next decision. A long document or a meeting count cannot supply all of those
facts. Different organizations may value different combinations of reliability,
speed, cost, innovation and participation; no common success index is imposed.

The earlier [issue-response pilot](../organizational-feedback/README.md) remains
a separate historical exercise. It has not been recoded as coordination data.
Youth and adult learning protocols are also unchanged. No organizational access,
interviews, institutional partnership or research approval is implied here.

AI assisted source discovery, extraction, interpretation and drafting. The
researcher and a second human reader still need to check the source-to-record
mapping and decide whether this is a useful observation unit.
