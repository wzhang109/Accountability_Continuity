## Candidate measures to discuss, not findings

For a later study, consider successful contact, time and repeated explanation,
resolution or a justified next step, user understanding, and privacy/access
costs. These concern the stated purpose of this service. They are not a standard
all organizations should share and have not been adopted or tested here.

Finding information, receiving personal help, exercising judgment and retaining
a skill are different outcomes. The present public record cannot replace a
human-learning study. Organizational structure, incentives and practical
review conditions likewise remain unobserved in this case.
