## What this changes in the question

The present project needs to distinguish accepting an offered correction from
being able to use the relevant distinction again. A sign-off or revised answer
cannot do both jobs. The audit separates three candidate observations: an
initial/final decision, a later unsupported decision, and the person's stated
reason. The third would still be partial evidence about reasoning, not a record
of everything considered.

For the classification-review question in RESEARCH_PROGRAM, identifying the
right choice from a supplied pair also differs from finding an overlooked
category. A proposal could record which alternatives are supplied, which the
reviewer introduces, what evidence separates them, and when a subsequent
unaided task occurs. These are questions to review, not an adopted instrument.
This source does not provide that missing alternative-generation trace.

## Corrections and comparison limits

Read records GM06-GM12 before using the earlier literature summary. A
nonsignificant contrast is not an estimate of exactly zero learning. Performance
on an immediate post-test, durable retention and organizational reuse require
different evidence. Equal feedback in a future experiment would remove one
source of difference; it would not by itself isolate timing from effort,
commitment or other workflow changes.

The three AI designs are not directly randomized against each other in this
paper. Evidence presentation, source framing, feedback and visual salience also
need attention when drawing a mechanism claim. The present reading cannot tell
which element caused a difference. It cannot establish that a workplace reviewer
has the authority, time or motivation to apply a correction.

The age and denominator questions remain open for human/version review. Do not
aggregate a replication sample or label the entire study adult-only. Do not
infer a youth-specific effect from an age range.
