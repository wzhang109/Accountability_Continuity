# Six baseline examples: review worksheet

**October 10, 2026 · fictional development material, not approved for administration**

This answer-free worksheet is for reviewing the proposed starting assessment.
The design is for ages 15–17, but the wording and difficulty have not been tested
with that age group. No participants have seen these materials.

Instructions proposed for review: use only the record for each item. Choose
**Supported**, **Contradicted**, or **Not enough information**, then give a short
reason. Supported means the record supports the claim; Contradicted means the
record conflicts with it; Not enough information means the record does not settle
it. You may skip an item. Confidence, if collected, is a number from 0 to 100
indicating how likely you think your selected answer is correct. It is not a grade.

Everyone would complete this block without AI advice and before allocation.
Answers and explanations would be recorded before any block-end feedback. The
existing feedback timing remains a provisional design choice. Calculator access,
language, timing and a neutral interface familiarization task still need review.
No claim is made that the six items constitute a validated judgment test.
