# Source selection for the organizational feedback pilot

**Search and coding date: October 1, 2026. Exploratory selection, not a systematic review.**

## Search route

Initial searches were:

- `site.gov.uk generative AI framework civil servants Copilot experiment evaluation feedback errors 2025`
- `site.research.google Understanding the Role of AI in Real-World Clinical Settings Thailand diabetic retinopathy feedback Beede 2020`

The first led to the GDS cross-government report, DWP evaluation, and original GOV.UK Chat experiment post. Links from the original Chat post led to the March, May, and August 2026 publications. Those publication dates were checked against the pages, rather than search-engine relative timestamps.

## Included records

S01 supplies early context; S02–S04 supply related accounts of one public service; S05 supplies an internal-workplace contrast. Only S02, S04, and S05 support episode-level claims in this first coding pass. S03 explains reported development practices and is not a sixth independent case. All five are first-party accounts and may be selective. No private correspondence, networking conversation, or participant data was used.

The source register records the exact URL, publication date, reading scope, a short locator excerpt, and file hashes. Cached HTML and paragraph-numbered text are kept outside the repository as working material. Hashes identify the retrieved files; they do not constitute an independent archive or establish that the publisher never changed the page. Public locators use section descriptions as well as local paragraph numbers.

- Beede and colleagues' 2020 Thai clinical AI study: original publication page and abstract inspected; full paper not read in this pass. Excluded from the present coding rather than presenting abstract-only reading as a completed case review. [Publication page](https://research.google/pubs/a-human-centered-evaluation-of-a-deep-learning-system-deployed-in-clinics-for-the-detection-of-diabetic-retinopathy/)
- NIST guidance and the Raji et al. abstract: conceptual context only; not implementation evidence.

## Selection limits and next search

This English-language search favors visible, well-documented government deployments. It does not represent private firms, the US, China, unsuccessful unpublished projects, or the full accountability literature. Entries were selected after reading the material, so this is exploratory coding, not a preregistered test.

The next search should deliberately seek linked response records and disconfirming cases, including appropriate refusals to change, unresolved reports, and changes with adverse or null outcomes. More publications about a single program cannot substitute for more independent settings.
