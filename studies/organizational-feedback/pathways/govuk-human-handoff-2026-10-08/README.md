# A human-support need and its public response history

**October 8, 2026 · AI-assisted descriptive reading · Human review pending**

This follows the existing pilot's **E02**, the need for personal human support
that GOV.UK Chat could not provide. It is one feature-level history, not a new
organization or an identified internal incident. The original five pilot
entries keep their dated classifications.

## What was added

Three source scopes were refreshed and three original publications were added:
a transparency record, a launch announcement and a technical-support manual.
The new reading includes one official public reply. This supplies a traceable
response statement while preserving the missing approval and outcome records.

## Purpose, measurement and authority

The [transparency record](https://www.gov.uk/algorithmic-transparency-records/dsit-gov-dot-uk-chat)
provides stated purpose and a program owner label. It does not establish who
could approve this feature or revise the service's goals. Its metadata date and
Private Beta phase must not be substituted for current service conditions.

The [engineering account](https://insidegovuk.blog.gov.uk/2026/05/15/developing-gov-uk-chat-our-data-science-and-ai-engineering-journey/)
provides general quality criteria. Those criteria do not supply a human-contact
outcome. Proposed follow-up measures in the data are analyst suggestions,
not measures demonstrated to have been used by this team.

The [developer support page](https://docs.publishing.service.gov.uk/manual/govuk-chat.html)
uses escalation to describe technical support. It is a scope check, not evidence
that a citizen can transfer to a departmental advisor. Its linked support
Google Document was not read.

## What this permits

A need and a publicly available exploration response can be described.
Implementation, a feature-linked reassessment, goal revision and feedback to
original users cannot yet be established from these records. Missing evidence
does not show inactivity. No effect on judgment, learning or accountability is
estimated, and no universal organizational score is constructed.

This is a useful unresolved record. It helps specify what additional evidence
would allow the research to move from an announced response to an observed
change and its consequences. See the [gap and review list](GAPS_AND_REVIEW.md).
