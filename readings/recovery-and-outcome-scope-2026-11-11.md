## A more precise observation question

The [November 9 contrast](non-adoption-and-criteria-2026-11-09.md) separated a
decision not to adopt from a request nobody received. Now ask: **after a decision,
what exactly changed, and which purpose does the observed result address?**

| Separate observations | Why keep them separate? |
|---|---|
| Decision and execution | Permission does not establish access to the necessary tool. |
| Execution and a scoped outcome | A recorded action does not establish its effect on every affected function. |
| Immediate restoration and later prevention | Announced work is not verified lasting improvement. |

Keep each event's source, time precision, affected function and unknown costs.
Do not infer an individual's exclusive decision right from a role or a meeting.
An operational indicator can address one purpose while leaving another open.

These are proposed recording distinctions for researcher review. This incident
does not supply an ordinary-day comparison, measured customer welfare, a causal
AI effect or a validated score. It does not change the evidence-access pilot or
add participants. AI assisted retrieval, interpretation and writing; researcher
understanding and independent source/method review remain pending.
