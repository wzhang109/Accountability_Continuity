# A work transition to examine

**Proposed recording aid, not a validated measure or a deployed study.**

Choose one bounded deliverable and one transition between roles. Do not begin
by assuming an AI-caused failure, a beneficial redesign or a preferred structure.
Use public evidence or material the researcher is permitted to use. Private
work records and personal recollections are not automatically research data or
publication-ready; permission, confidentiality and applicable review must be
settled before collection or sharing.

| Record | What to enter | Evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Deliverable and purpose | What must be delivered, to whom, and what success means in this setting | Distinguish official target, another role's target and the researcher's interpretation |
| Scope and time | Start/end boundary; source date and observation window | Do not invent timestamps or regularity from one account |
| Roles and tasks | Who was expected to do what; any actual reassignment | Do not infer actual work from a job title |
| Required input | What the next step depended on, from which role or system | Separate stated dependency from demonstrated blockage |
| Handoff requirement | What had to be understood, checked or supplied for acceptance | Identify the source of the standard and disagreement about it |
| Actual sequence | What happened before and after this transition | Mark protocol order, reported sequence and logged events differently |
| Waiting or rework | Start/stop evidence, reason and role bearing the burden | Missing timing stays unknown; elapsed time is not all active labor |
| Knowledge and decision rights | Who knew the relevant facts and who could authorize the next action | Submission, recommendation and approval are separate actions |
| Tradeoff and resolution | Competing goals or constraints, decision and basis, if documented | Do not infer shared goals or a universal best decision |
| AI contribution | Specific input/output, user, model/date if known, and checks performed | Separate access from use and text reuse from lack of judgment |
| Work outcomes | Setting-specific quality, reliability, time or resources actually observed | Label forecasts and ratings; do not turn them into realized value |
| Role changes | Responsibilities gained/lost and information needed for the next action | Do not infer a change in actual work from a title or a better output |
| Evidence and alternatives | Source/locator; report versus observation; rival explanations and unknowns | Changes in people, workload, authority or standards may accompany AI use |

Use `unknown` with a reason when an expected field cannot be established.
An unknown is a finding about this reading's evidence coverage, not proof that
the organization lacks a process. Leave numerical fields empty when unobserved;
do not convert missing duration, cost or outcomes to zero.

Compare a transition with its stated requirement before comparing organizations.
If comparing AI use, first document whether task, personnel, resources, incentives
and decision rights also changed. A before/after narrative alone does not isolate
an AI effect. One-off pairs and continuing teams are different settings.

This template creates neither a universal coordination score nor recruitment
permission. Researcher review should first decide whether one genuine work
episode or one public process record can support the fields. No person or
organization has been contacted for this purpose.
