# What counts as good when an organization uses AI

**Wenwen (Celine) Zhang · Working reflection · October 1, 2026**

This note develops a philosophical motivation for Accountability Continuity. It
does not report a new empirical finding. The reflection arose in conversation
and was developed and written with substantial AI assistance.

## The question before the measure

If we do not actively consider what a good life means to us, readily available
measures can begin to stand in for that judgment. Income, speed, and recognition
can tell us something useful without telling us everything we care about.

I see a related question in organizations adopting AI: who decides what counts
as a good outcome, and can that definition be reconsidered when people encounter
its consequences? Before asking whether a tool performs well, we have already
made choices about which purposes matter and whose experience will count.

Consider a hypothetical school. Faster completion of assignments and a student's
growing ability to understand a problem could both be valuable. They need not
improve together. Measuring completion time would tell us something about one
aim; it would not settle how the school should balance them.

## What I mean by returning to the heart

In Chinese, I have described this as returning to “心”: attending to what we care
about, why we care, and what responsibility we accept for our choices. I am using
the term to express a concern, not to introduce a measurable psychological trait
or a claim about an essential difference between humans and machines.

That concern includes being willing to listen and to be changed by experience.
Sincere intentions can still have consequences we did not anticipate. Judgment
therefore needs contact with those consequences and with the people who bear them.
An intuition deserves examination; it does not become authoritative merely
because it feels deeply held.

## Different purposes and shared questions

No single metric can define good AI use for every organization. That does not
make every organizational preference equally justified. Within one institution,
leaders, staff, and the people it serves may also disagree about what matters.
A declared mission does not establish that those disagreements have been heard.

Across different purposes, we can still ask who chooses the goals, who can
question them, whose experience becomes visible, and who has the ability and
responsibility to respond. Keeping these questions open matters both when an
AI-assisted process misses its target and when meeting the target reveals a
cost the organization had overlooked.

## How this extends the research

The organizational branch currently traces reported issues and responses. This
reflection adds a question for future evidence collection: did the response
improve performance against an existing aim, or did experience lead people to
reconsider the aim or its measure? Both could matter. Neither is inherently the
better response in every situation.

The five public-document entries do not yet establish who legitimately defined
success, whose views were excluded, or whether a goal itself changed. They have
not been retroactively coded as evidence for this reflection. The simulation
and proposed learning experiment likewise do not settle what a good life or
good organizational purpose should be.

The broader AC question can now be stated as follows: **as AI takes on more work,
can people and organizations continue to participate in defining what is worth
pursuing, encounter the consequences of their choices, and revise their judgment
in light of what they learn?**

This remains a motivation and a set of questions. The next contribution must
come from specifying which part can be observed, considering other explanations,
and learning from practice.

## 中文说明

“回归到心”在这里指的是：认真对待我们在乎什么、为什么在乎，以及愿意
为自己的选择承担什么。组织使用 AI 之前，已经在选择哪些目标值得追求、
哪些结果值得衡量。指标可以帮助我们判断，却不能替我们完成对目的的思考。

不同组织未必追求同一种“好”，同一个组织内部也可能存在分歧。因此，研究
还要关心谁能参与定义目标，谁的经验会被听见，以及发现实际后果以后，
人们是否有机会、有能力修正目标或做法。“心”也需要通过倾听和实践继续成长。

这为 AC 增加了关于目的与价值的提问，仍属于哲学动机。今天整理的案例、
已有模拟和未实施的学习实验，各自只能回答其中有限的问题。我们将继续
分开记录思想、可检验的问题与实际证据。
