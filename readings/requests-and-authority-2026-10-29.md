# When obtaining evidence needs authorization

**October 29, 2026 · AI-assisted reading; human review pending**

The [CAIB report, Volume I](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/static/history/columbia/reports/CAIBreportv1.pdf)
records requests for additional imagery, activity to identify imaging resources,
and cancellation (pp. 150–154; summary pp. 166–167). The Board distinguishes
engineering requests from what managers recognized as an operational requirement.
It also describes uncertainty about imaging capability and cost. The
[episode index](imagery-request-episode-2026-10-29.json) preserves which details
come from reproduced records, retrospective reconstruction and interpretation.

This changes the next question after the [drawing-and-redesign reading](drawing-and-redesign-2026-10-27.md):
before asking whether people share a usable representation, ask who can obtain
missing evidence and authorize the next action. A written request or a shared
concern does not establish that the relevant decision-maker received and
understood it, or recognized authority to act.

| Proposed distinction | What this source permits | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| An information need versus an authorized action | Requests and cancellation are traceable | Everyone shared a complete understanding |
| A stated resource concern versus checked feasibility | Concerns were reported; the Board found capability/cost knowledge missing | A measured cost that explains the decision |
| An action outcome versus a safety counterfactual | Imagery activity was halted | What obtaining imagery would have changed |

The event makes a representation-only diagnosis insufficiently specified; it
does not isolate an authority effect. Misunderstanding, prioritization and
expertise remain competing or interacting explanations. This exceptional,
retrospective accident account is not a representative sample of routine
organizing. The strict counterexample remains **not established**.

A later comparison should look for requests with documented recipients,
authorization and feasible alternatives, including approved requests that did
not resolve the work. That could weaken an explanation based only on routing
or approval. These are observation questions, not a universal sequence or
validated scale. No AI intervention or original field data is added. See the
[source record](sources-2026-10-29.json) for the bounded reading scope.
