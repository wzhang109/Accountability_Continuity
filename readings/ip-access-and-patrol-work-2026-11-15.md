# IP access and everyday patrol work on English Wikipedia

This source reading follows a routine job: investigating a potentially abusive edit far enough to decide whether to report or act. English Wikipedia’s temporary accounts changed what an editor sees when trying to connect edits across accounts. The case makes it possible to ask who can set an access rule, apply it, grant an exception, and do the investigation when a clue is available only to some people. The change studied here is the temporary-account system and its permissions.

## Why closure time and work can move separately

The Foundation’s April 2026 English Wikipedia survey asked about experiences after deployment and had no pre-deployment survey baseline. Some authorized respondents described better access to patterns across temporary accounts. Other respondents described extra tracking work, fragmented histories and trouble following a user across accounts. The report also summarizes lower perceived ease for some respondents alongside confidence in the community’s ability to handle vandalism. The reported opinions are evidence about participants’ experience. The report flags tool-name confusion, and its overall response total conflicts with the complete and partial counts on the same page. This reading does not use those totals to calculate a response rate or estimate prevalence. [Claudia Lo, Foundation survey report, pp. 2–6, 13, 17–24, 26](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:(Public_release)_WE_4.8.3_Post-TA_enwiki_patroller_sentiments_-_Findings.pdf)

The Foundation’s separate log analysis measured elapsed time from filing to closure for Sockpuppet Investigations and examined report volume for Administrator Intervention against Vandalism. It did not attribute a change in closure time to temporary accounts and reported no evidence of an increased report rate in the second venue. Repeated investigation before filing, an extra handoff to an authorized user, or more time spent actively tracing edits could all escape those measures. These are possible explanations for the divergence in what the sources describe. The reading did not establish that any of them occurred in an individual case. [Foundation log-analysis ticket, February 6 comment](https://phabricator.wikimedia.org/T415103)

## What this changes in the proposed observation

The [November 13 editor reading](position-and-shortcut-2026-11-13.md) separated the task, route, version and environment. In this case the same task and route can also depend on the role’s permission at the time. For a future work record, capture who can access the clue, who grants or overrides access, and who actually handles each evidentiary step. Keep elapsed waiting time and active work time separate. Where access is restricted, a role-level observation can use permitted public actions or aggregate evidence without recording the underlying IP.

The sources do not resolve effects on privacy incidents, missed abuse, unnecessary blocks, work per role, or time spent before filing. This is an AI-assisted reading of public decisions, attributed reports and summaries. Researcher reading, independent source checking and method review are pending.
